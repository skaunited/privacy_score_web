Chapter 02 / 05 The product

A score out of 100.
Three concrete actions.
Zero scare tactics.

Privacy Score reads the App Privacy Report that iOS is already generating on your iPhone. It cross-references every domain your apps contact with the open DuckDuckGo Tracker Radar database, then calculates a score, and tells you exactly what to fix.

  • check Reads Apple's native report (no extra permissions).
  • check Calculates the score on-device, in 2 seconds.
  • check Surfaces 3 to 5 fixes you can apply right now.
  • check Tracks your progress week after week.

Frequently asked

What to know before your first audit.

What does Apple’s report show? expand_more

Data and sensor access, plus domains contacted by apps and websites during the recorded period. It does not reveal request contents. See Apple’s documentation.

Is the first audit free? expand_more

Your first audit is free. Premium includes additional audits, DNS/VPN tools and report export. Annual: €23.88 for the first year, then €59.99/year. Six months: €20.94 for the first six months, then €39.99/six months. Current French App Store prices for eligible subscriptions; Apple confirms eligibility, local prices and renewal terms before purchase.

What if my report is empty? expand_more

Recording starts after you enable it. Use your apps and check again. Do not turn the report off to refresh it: that clears recorded data. Read the troubleshooting guide.

Does a contacted domain prove tracking? expand_more

No. A connection can support content, authentication, notifications or measurement. Consider the domain, its context and the limits of its classification before drawing a conclusion.

Which devices are supported? expand_more

Privacy Score is available for iPhone and iPad running iOS/iPadOS 17.6 or later. The app and website support English and French. Check the App Store listing for your device’s compatibility.
