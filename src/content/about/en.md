Privacy Score is built by Skander Bahri, an independent iOS developer based in Bordeaux, France, after a single 2024 story: reporters at Wired and 404 Media bought a commercial advertising dataset, and inside it they identified <a href="https://www.wired.com/story/secret-service-phone-location-data-babel-street/" rel="external">26 Secret Service agents</a> guarding the White House. No leak. No hack. A legal data-broker purchase, powered by ordinary advertising signals on ordinary iPhones. That story made the point uncomfortably clear: the privacy problem on a 2024 iPhone wasn't a spy agency, it was the ad-tech industry operating perfectly legally. The fix, if there was one, had to come from individual users tightening their own iPhones, one permission at a time.

## What CoDevelop is

CoDevelop is a one-person micro-studio registered in France, run by Skander. It exists to make honest privacy tools that don't ask you to trust them on faith. The pattern most privacy apps land on is uncomfortable: they read your data, route it through their servers, and promise they won't keep anything. You have to believe them. CoDevelop is built on a different premise: the only credible privacy promise is one you can verify yourself, on your own device, without taking anyone's word for it.

## What Privacy Score refuses to do

Privacy Score does one thing: it reads <a href="https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iphd34a4cdf7/ios" rel="external">Apple's native App Privacy Report</a>, cross-references the domains against the open DuckDuckGo Tracker Radar database, computes a score out of 100, and surfaces three to five concrete fixes ranked by how many points they earn back. That's it.

Three things it refuses to do, on purpose:

- **No server.** Zero backend infrastructure. Your privacy report stays on your iPhone, inside the iOS sandbox.
- **No account.** No sign-up, no email, no password. Nothing about your usage can be tied back to you, because nothing about your usage is collected.
- **No analytics.** No Firebase, no Sentry, no Amplitude, no "anonymized" usage reporting (which is rarely as anonymized as the marketing copy suggests).

You don't have to take our word for any of this. Turn on Airplane Mode and Privacy Score keeps working, because everything happens on your device. And in your own iOS App Privacy Report, Privacy Score shows zero contacted domains. The promise is verifiable, on your own iPhone.

## Why the first scan is free for life

Trust before money. If we charged a subscription before you'd ever seen the app work, you'd be picking us based on marketing claims. We don't want that. The first time you import an App Privacy Report and read your score, that audit is free for life, and stays accessible on your iPhone for as long as you keep the app installed. You see the value, then you decide whether to keep auditing yourself regularly.

The paid tier is €23.88 per year or €20.94 per six months, billed by Apple. That's our only source of revenue. No advertising, no data resale, no partner commissions.
