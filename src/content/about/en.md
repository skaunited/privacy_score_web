Privacy Score is an iPhone app built by **Skander Bahri**, the independent developer behind CoDevelop in France. Its purpose is practical: help you understand recorded app activity and decide which privacy settings deserve your attention.

## What the app analyzes

You choose when to export an [Apple App Privacy Report](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102188) and open it in Privacy Score. The app analyzes that file locally on your iPhone, adds context about contacted domains, and presents a score and recommendations. No Privacy Score account is required.

The report is an observation of activity. It does not reveal the contents of encrypted requests, establish that every domain is a tracker, or certify that an app is safe. The score is an internal indicator to help organize a review, rather than an Apple rating or a security certification.

Privacy Score also provides Premium tools for DNS/VPN configuration and export. Those tools are separate from importing a report: filtering depends on the configuration you approve and the provider you use. Read the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/) for the app's data handling and the website's separate practices.

## Free analysis and optional Premium

The first report analysis is free. Premium supports continued use and additional features through Apple in-app purchases. The local purchase screen provides the applicable price, subscription period, renewal terms, and any introductory-offer eligibility before you subscribe.

For eligible customers in the French App Store, the annual launch offer is **€23.88 for the first year**, followed by the **standard annual price of €59.99** at renewal. Prices and offers can differ by storefront; the offer is not a permanent worldwide annual price.

[View Privacy Score on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6762229573).

## Guides and corrections

The [blog](/blog/) explains Apple's controls, report export, and common interpretation issues. Articles link to the sources used and show their revision date. Documentation checks are not presented as device tests; when a procedure depends on iOS version or region, that limitation matters.

If a guide contains an error or an app feature does not behave as described, contact [support@privacyscore.fr](mailto:support@privacyscore.fr). Include the page URL or app version and a short description. Please describe the problem before sending a complete privacy report, which can contain personal activity metadata.
