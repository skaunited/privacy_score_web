## Anti-keywords (DO NOT TARGET)

- ❌ "free iphone unlock" — irrelevant, attracts wrong audience
- ❌ "iphone spy app" — legal risk, brand damage
- ❌ "icloud password recovery" — adjacent but wrong intent

## SEO copy guidelines per language

### FR
- Use `vous` (formal you)
- Reference CNIL, RGPD, Loi Informatique et Libertés
- Cite French press: Numerama, Frandroid, Le Monde, 01net
- Avoid English loanwords when a French equivalent exists ("le téléchargement" not "le download")

### EN
- Direct, conversational tone
- Reference GDPR, CCPA, FTC
- Cite English tech press: TechCrunch, The Verge, Wired, ArsTechnica
- US English by default; UK spelling only on `/en-gb/` if we add it later
